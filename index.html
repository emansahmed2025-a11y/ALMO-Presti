01THE MAISON

WHERE ENGINEERING MEETS LUXURY.

ALMO Prestige treats a frame the way an architect treats a façade — nothing decorative survives unless it carries a load. Every silhouette begins as a technical drawing, is stress-tested in CAD, and only then earns the right to be cut from titanium.

The maison was founded on a single conviction: luxury is not ornament, it is tolerance. A hinge that outlives its owner, a finish applied in eleven passes, a serial number engraved where only you will find it.

    05LIMITED EDITION

    THEVANGUARD.

    TWENTY-FIVE PIECES. EVER.

    The Vanguard is cut from a single sheet of Grade-5 titanium and folded — not soldered — into form. Twenty-five pieces will exist. When the final piece is claimed, the drawing is retired to the archive and the mould is destroyed.

    Each piece is individually numbered on the inner temple and accompanied by a numbered certificate of authenticity.

    Select an open number to reserve that exact piece.

    10CARE GUIDE

    MAINTAIN THESTANDARD.

    1. 01

      Rinse First

      Rinse frames in lukewarm water before wiping — dust is abrasive.

    2. 02

      The Right Cloth

      Microfibre only. Shirts and tissues micro-scratch the coating.

    3. 03

      Heat Is the Enemy

      Never leave frames on a sunlit dashboard. Titanium survives; the lenses will not.

    4. 04

      Rest It Cased

      Store closed, temples folded, lenses facing up.

    5. 05

      Periodic Torque Check

      Bring the frame in once a year for a complimentary hinge check and re-torque.
